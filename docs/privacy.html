Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Overview

Tweeticcini provides subscription software for Discord server owners who want to manage Twitter/X alert routing, filtering, and server-specific notification rules.

Information we process

To operate the service, Tweeticcini may process limited information such as:

  • Discord server identifiers, channel identifiers, and role identifiers
  • Configured Twitter/X source usernames
  • Guild-level settings, alert rules, and presentation preferences
  • Billing-related metadata provided through payment processors
  • Operational logs and diagnostic information used to maintain the service

How information is used

  • To deliver configured alerts into Discord
  • To store and apply source routing, filtering, and escalation rules
  • To maintain account security, service reliability, and abuse prevention
  • To administer subscriptions and customer support

Third-party services

Tweeticcini relies on third-party services including Discord, Twitter/X-related tooling, hosting providers, and payment providers such as Stripe. Information may be processed by those providers as required to operate the service.

Data retention

Configuration data, logs, and billing-related metadata may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain reliability, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Contact

During limited launch, privacy and support requests are handled directly through the service operator for onboarded customers.
